Key findings

  • Bacterial notifications rose +32% between 2021 and 2025 and are +23% above 2019. Main contributors: Shigellosis, Gonorrhoea, Lyme borreliosis.
  • Viral notifications excluding COVID-19 changed +1213%, driven mainly by influenza, which was almost absent in 2021 (440 cases) during pandemic measures.
  • Excluding influenza as well, viral notifications are -4% vs 2019: the post-COVID viral rise is mostly an influenza rebound, while the bacterial rise is broad.
  • Largest relative increases 2021→2025: Influenza +7851%; Shigellosis +134%; Hepatitis A +74%.
  • Largest decreases: COVID-19 -98%; Hepatitis E -48%.
